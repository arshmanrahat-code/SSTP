Admissions Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common enrollment queries regarding fees, certificates, and class schedules.

Are scholarships or fee waivers available? keyboard_arrow_down
Yes. Through SHED Foundation's endowment and community partnerships, merit and need-based fee waivers are available for deserving applicants. Indicate your scholarship request during your initial on-campus interview.
What certificates are awarded upon completion? keyboard_arrow_down
Graduates receive an accredited Technical Competency Diploma issued by SHED Skills Training Program, recognized by provincial technical education boards and verified through our digital badge registry.
Is job placement assistance guaranteed? keyboard_arrow_down
While employment is subject to market demand and student academic standing, SHED operates an active Career & Placement Cell with over 45 industry partners across solar, IT, and HVAC sectors for direct apprenticeships.
Can working professionals attend evening or weekend classes? keyboard_arrow_down
Yes. We offer specialized Weekend and Evening Executive batches for our Digital Skills, Solar Design, and Electrical modules specifically tailored for working technicians and professionals.
