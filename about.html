Learning That Supports Personal and Professional Growth

The SHED Skills Training Program is focused on providing information about skills development and training opportunities. This website helps visitors explore the program, learn about potential training areas, and find the right way to make an inquiry.

We believe that learning practical skills can help individuals strengthen their abilities, build confidence, and prepare for different educational and professional pathways.

Our Direction

Our Mission and Vision

Our approach is built around learning, practical development, and helping people explore opportunities for the future.

visibility

Our Vision

To encourage a future where individuals have opportunities to develop useful skills, improve their knowledge, and work toward personal growth and sustainable livelihoods.

track_changes

Our Mission

To promote skills development and accessible learning by helping interested individuals discover training opportunities and information that supports their educational and professional goals.

What Guides Us

Our Core Values

These principles guide the way we present learning opportunities and support the goals of prospective learners.

diversity_3

Inclusion

Encouraging respectful learning and opportunities for people from different backgrounds.

handshake

Integrity

Promoting honesty, respect, responsibility, and clear communication with learners.

Quality

Learning Approach

A Journey from Learning to Application

A simple framework for understanding how skills can be developed and applied in everyday and professional settings.

01

Discover

Explore your interests, identify learning goals, and learn about relevant training options.

02

Learn

Build knowledge and understand the foundations of a chosen skill or subject.

03

Practice

Apply what you learn through suitable exercises, activities, or practical projects.

04

Progress

Continue improving your abilities and explore relevant next steps for your future.
